# 📱 WhatsApp Business API - Setup Checklist

Follow this step-by-step checklist. Each step takes 2-5 minutes.

---

## ☐ Step 1: Create Meta Business Account (5 min)

1. **Open:** https://business.facebook.com/overview
2. Click **"Create Account"**
3. Fill in:
   - Business Name: `TechyEra` (or your name)
   - Your Name: Your full name
   - Business Email: Your email
4. Check your email and **verify**

✅ Done? Move to Step 2

---

## ☐ Step 2: Create Developer App (3 min)

1. **Open:** https://developers.facebook.com/apps/
2. Login with same Facebook account
3. Click **"Create App"**
4. Select: **"Other"** → **"Next"**
5. Select: **"Business"** → **"Next"**
6. Fill in:
   - App Name: `WhatsApp Marketing`
   - Contact Email: Your email
   - Business Account: Select from dropdown
7. Click **"Create App"**

✅ Done? Move to Step 3

---

## ☐ Step 3: Add WhatsApp to Your App (2 min)

1. In App Dashboard, scroll down to **"Add Products"**
2. Find **"WhatsApp"** → Click **"Set Up"**
3. Click **"Continue"**
4. You're now on **WhatsApp API Setup** page

✅ Done? Move to Step 4

---

## ☐ Step 4: Copy Your Credentials (2 min)

On the WhatsApp > API Setup page:

### 4a. Phone Number ID
- Look for dropdown: **"From"** 
- Below it shows: `Phone number ID: XXXXXXXXXXXXX`
- **Copy this number**

### 4b. Access Token
- Click **"Generate"** next to Temporary access token
- A long token starting with `EAAG...` appears
- **Copy this token**

### 4c. WhatsApp Business Account ID
- Look at the URL in browser: `...waba_id=XXXXXXXXXXXXX`
- Or go to: WhatsApp > API Setup > scroll to see WABA ID
- **Copy this ID**

## ☐ Step 6 (Optional): Add Your Business Phone

If you want to use your own phone number instead of test number:

1. Go to WhatsApp > API Setup
2. Click **"Add phone number"**
3. Enter your WhatsApp Business phone
4. Verify with OTP
5. Complete **Business Verification** (requires PAN/GST)
