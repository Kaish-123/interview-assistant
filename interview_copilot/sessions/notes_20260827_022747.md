# Interview Summary Notes

## Key Questions Asked
1. How do you manage version differences in code or software?
2. What strategies do you use for managing real-time data?
3. How do you ensure data quality?
4. How do you prevent quality rules from becoming stale with changing business logic?
5. What steps do you take when dealing with schema drift and pipeline failures?
6. How do you protect data integrity when ingesting market files into Delta layers?
7. What measures do you take to prevent data leakage through aggregates or exports?
8. What design or operational choices help control costs on a cloud data platform?
9. How would you identify and manage cost drivers related to inefficient file handling?

## Topics Covered
- Version control and backward compatibility in code.
- Strategies for real-time data management.
- Data quality assurance processes.
- Maintaining relevance of quality rules amidst changing business logic.
- Handling schema drift and errors in data pipelines.
- Data protection during ingestion into Delta layers.
- Preventing data leakage in exports and aggregates.
- Cost management strategies in cloud data platforms.
- Identifying and managing inefficient data files.

## Strong Answer Points
- Emphasis on **data validation**, **monitoring**, and **documentation** for managing version differences and real-time data.
- Utilization of **automated validation** and **data cleansing** to ensure high data quality.
- Implementation of **rollback mechanisms** and **schema validation** to handle schema drift effectively.
- Adoption of **data masking** and **strict access controls** to prevent data leakage.
- Use of **auto-scaling**, **data lifecycle management**, and **cost allocation** for controlling cloud spending.
- Application of **data profiling** and **automated quality checks** to manage cost drivers related to file handling.

## Gaps/Follow-Ups
- Further clarification on specific tools or technologies used for real-time data processing and validation.
- Examples of past experiences where these strategies were successfully implemented.
- Discussion on how to handle unexpected spikes in data costs and the team's response protocols.

## Action Items
- Prepare examples of past projects that illustrate the application of discussed strategies.
- Research and compile a list of specific tools and technologies relevant to real-time data management and cloud cost control.
- Schedule a follow-up meeting to discuss the implementation of these strategies in a practical context.
