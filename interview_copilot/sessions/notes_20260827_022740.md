# Interview Summary Notes

## Key Questions Asked
1. How do you manage version differences in code or software?
2. What strategies do you employ for managing real-time data?
3. How do you focus on data quality in your projects?
4. How do you prevent quality rules from becoming stale with changing business logic?
5. What steps do you take when dealing with schema drift and pipeline failures?
6. How do you protect data integrity when ingesting market files into Delta layers?
7. What measures do you take to prevent data leakage through aggregates or exports?
8. What design or operational choices help control costs on a cloud data platform?
9. How do you identify cost drivers like excessive refreshes or inefficient file sizes?

## Topics Covered
- Version control and backward compatibility
- Real-time data management strategies
- Data quality assurance methods
- Maintaining relevance of data quality rules
- Schema drift management
- Data protection during ingestion
- Data leakage prevention strategies
- Cost management on cloud platforms
- Identifying and managing cost drivers

## Strong Answer Points
- **Version Differences**: Emphasized backward compatibility, testing, documentation, and collaboration.
- **Real-Time Data Management**: Discussed data validation, stream processing, monitoring, and scalability.
- **Data Quality Focus**: Highlighted data profiling, establishing standards, automated validation, and continuous improvement.
- **Preventing Stale Quality Rules**: Mentioned regular reviews, stakeholder collaboration, and documentation.
- **Schema Drift Handling**: Explained error logging, rollback mechanisms, and continuous monitoring.
- **Data Integrity in Delta Layers**: Detailed validation checks, schema enforcement, and audit logging.
- **Cost Control on Cloud Platforms**: Shared strategies like right-sizing resources, auto-scaling, and data lifecycle management.
